Anisio Lemos de Moraes

Software developer

I am a philosophy teacher turned software developer with a passion for solving problems that make a positive impact on people's lives.

I've always loved computers and have been using Linux since I was a kid, but I didn't choose Computer Science as my major because I feared math.

I ended up in a second-semester logic class, learning that I didn't fear math, I just hated school. That made me more motivated to become a good teacher to make things different.

Life went on, the pandemic hit the world, and things turned upside down. When I had no hope of things getting better for me, almost by chance, I became a software developer at C6 Bank.

I want to use this site to improve my communication skills and document the things that I code and don't want to forget. Not tutorials, just my process.

After four years away from college and without a routine of reading, thinking deeply while bored, and writing with a purpose in mind, I'm missing that.
